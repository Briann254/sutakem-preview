      Questions

      Straight answers.

      Anything else? Call 0790 444 921.

      Is the site visit really free?

      Yes. We visit, look at your site and recommend a setup at no cost. You only pay if you go ahead with the installation.

      Can I watch my cameras from my phone?

      Yes. Our CCTV installations include 24/7 recording and remote viewing, so you can see live and recorded footage from your phone wherever you are.

      How many cameras will I need?

      It depends on your entrances, rooms and grounds, which is why we visit first. Use "Find your setup" above for a starting point, then we confirm the exact number on site.

      Do you work with homes or only businesses?

      Both. We protect homes, offices, schools, shops, estates and warehouses, and size every system to the site.

      Which ETR machines do you repair?

      Incotex 181, Incotex 600, Incotex 777 and Aclass. We also install ETR and eTIMS systems and connect them to QuickBooks, Zoho, Odoo or Sage.

      What happens after installation?

      You get 24/7 support. If something needs attention, call or WhatsApp us and the team that installed it responds.
