// questions

Straight answers.

Anything else? Call +254 790 444921, we pick up.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. We visit, assess your site and recommend a setup at no cost. You only pay if you go ahead with the installation.

Can I watch my cameras from my phone?

Yes. Our CCTV installations include 24/7 recording and remote monitoring, so you can view live and recorded footage from your phone wherever you are.

Do you work with homes or only businesses?

Both. We protect homes, offices, schools, shops, estates and warehouses, and size every system to the site.

Which ETR machines do you repair?

Incotex 181, Incotex 600, Incotex 777 and Aclass. We also install ETR and eTIMS systems and integrate them with your point of sale.

Can eTIMS connect to my accounting software?

Yes. We handle eTIMS compliance and ERP integration with QuickBooks, Zoho, Odoo and Sage.

What happens after installation?

You get 24/7 support. If something needs attention, call or WhatsApp us and a technician responds.
