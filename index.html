      Frequently Asked Questions

      Clear answers to common enquiries.

      Have a specific technical question? Speak directly with our team at 0790 444 921.

      1. Is the site assessment free of charge?

      Yes. We offer complimentary initial site assessments to understand your requirements, evaluate the premises, and recommend suitable solutions. A quotation is provided before any installation or paid work begins.

      2. Can I access my CCTV cameras remotely?

      Yes. Compatible CCTV systems can be configured to allow remote access through smartphones, tablets, or computers. This enables you to view live footage and access recorded videos remotely, subject to the system's configuration and internet connectivity.

      3. How many CCTV cameras will I need?

      The number of cameras depends on the size and layout of your premises, the areas requiring coverage, and your security objectives. Our technical team will assess your property during the complimentary assessment and recommend an appropriate configuration.

      4. Do you provide services to residential and commercial customers?

      Yes. We serve residential customers, businesses, schools, institutions, and other organisations. Our solutions are tailored to the specific requirements of each property or facility.

      5. Which ETR machines do you repair?

      We provide repair and technical support for selected ETR models, including Incotex 181, Incotex 600, Incotex 777, and Aclass. Contact our team to confirm support for your particular machine.

      6. Do you assist with eTIMS installation and integration?

      Yes. We assist with eTIMS installation, configuration, and integration with compatible POS and accounting platforms, including QuickBooks, Zoho, Odoo, and Sage, subject to compatibility.

      7. Do you provide maintenance after installation?

      Yes. We provide technical support and maintenance services for systems installed by our team, subject to the agreed service arrangements.

      8. Can you work with existing security and network systems?

      Yes. We can assess your existing installations and advise on maintenance, troubleshooting, upgrades, or integration with compatible equipment.

      9. How do I request a quotation?

      You can contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or email, or complete the site assessment request form on this website. Following an assessment of your requirements, we will provide a quotation outlining the recommended solution and associated costs.

      10. How can I contact Sutakem Solutions?

      You can reach our team directly via telephone at 0790 444 921, on WhatsApp, or via email at sutakemsolutions@gmail.com. We are ready to assist with your security and technology requirements.
